READ THIS FIRST

PixelSwap is a browser-local simulation built to show how this DEX would feel on the LitVM LiteForge testnet (chain 4441). It runs real constant-product AMM maths against a simulated pool in your browser. The reference prices are live mainnet rates fetched from Kraken, which is exactly what the testnet pools mirror.

No contracts are deployed on LiteForge, no real funds move, and no wallet signature is ever requested — the wallet, balances, faucet and keeper here are all simulated. PixelSwap is a concept prepared for the LitVM “Submit Your App” directory.
